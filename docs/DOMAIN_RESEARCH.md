# Telpen Adverts Domain Research

Date: 2026-06-16
Purpose: Shortlist marketable domain names around the platform promise: sell it, find it here, and connect.

## Recommendation

Buy `SellFindConnect.com` first.

Why:

- It directly captures the product command: sell, find, connect.
- It works for both advertisers and searchers.
- It is broad enough for suppliers, producers, consumers, clients, logistics partners, and service providers.
- It is easy to turn into a memorable campaign line: "Sell it. Find it. Connect."
- It does not trap the product as only classified ads, only sourcing, or only chat.
- RDAP returned 404/not found for `sellfindconnect.com`, which was a strong availability signal before checkout.
- `SellFindConnect.com` was purchased through GoDaddy on 2026-06-16.

Recommended defensive buys:

1. `SellFindConnect.com` - primary public domain.
2. `SellFindConnect.app` - mobile/PWA companion and app campaign domain.
3. `FindSellConnect.com` - protects the reverse search-first phrase.
4. `FindSellConnect.app` - protects the search-first app companion.

## Avoid

| Domain | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| `FindItConnect.com` | Registered through GoDaddy. |
| `FindItSellIt.com` | Registered through GoDaddy. |
| `SellIt.com` | Too generic and likely expensive or unavailable. |
| `Connect.com` | Too generic and likely expensive or unavailable. |
| Hyphenated variants | Harder to say, easier to mistype, weaker for mass-market advertising. |

## Buying Outcome And Next Steps

1. `SellFindConnect.com` was purchased through GoDaddy on 2026-06-16.
2. The selected term was 1 year. Before payment, GoDaddy showed domain registration at GBP 9.99, renewal in June 2027 at GBP 18.99, and Full Domain Protection at GBP 7.99.
3. Do not buy GoDaddy email until SPF, DKIM, DMARC, admin mailbox, support mailbox, and deliverability requirements are agreed.
4. Buy `SellFindConnect.app` if budget allows after the primary `.com` is secured.
5. If budget allows, also buy `FindSellConnect.com` and `FindSellConnect.app`.
6. Connect `SellFindConnect.com` to Railway after choosing how to handle Railway's Trial-plan custom domain limit.
7. Preferred path: upgrade Railway or otherwise increase the custom-domain limit so `adverts.telpen.net` can remain as the technical fallback while `SellFindConnect.com` is verified.
8. Budget path: remove/replace the existing `adverts.telpen.net` custom domain on the web service and use Railway's `web-production-32b7d.up.railway.app` URL as the technical fallback until a paid Railway plan is approved.
9. Redirect the new domain to the current Telpen deployment first, then later make it the primary domain after legal/trademark review.
10. Run a trademark and country-name-conflict search before heavy public advertising.
