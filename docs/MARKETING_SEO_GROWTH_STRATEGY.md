# Sell Find Connect Marketing, SEO, And Growth Strategy

Date: 2026-06-16
Brand domain: `SellFindConnect.com`
Campaign line: Sell it. Find it. Connect.

## Market Position

Sell Find Connect should be marketed as a commercial relationship intelligence
platform, not only as an advert board. The strongest promise is:

> Sell what you offer, find who supplies it, and connect with likely customers,
> suppliers, producers, distributors, and service providers.

This positioning gives the platform a sharper market niche than generic
classifieds, directories, marketplaces, and social media pages.

## Primary SEO Themes

Use these as the main content pillars across the website, app-store listings,

1. Source products and services locally.

## Technical SEO Requirements

- Public pages must be server-rendered or statically generated where practical.
- Create XML sitemaps for home, countries, industries, categories, profiles,
  and help articles.
- Add `robots.txt` allowing search engines to crawl public pages while blocking
  private dashboards, admin, checkout, chat, and account pages.
- Use canonical URLs on profile, category, country, and industry pages.
- Use structured data for Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, LocalBusiness
  where applicable, Product or Service where applicable, FAQPage for FAQs, and
  SoftwareApplication for the app page.
- Add Open Graph and Twitter/X card metadata to all public pages.
- Create clean URLs:
  - `/countries/kenya`
  - `/countries/kenya/agriculture`
  - `/industries/construction`
  - `/business/{country}/{slug}`
  - `/source-finder/{country}/{industry}`
- Add alt text to every public image.
- Compress and resize uploaded images and videos.
- Add Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics events, and conversion
  tracking only after privacy notices and cookie/consent handling are ready.

## Trust And Safety Marketing

Market safety as a serious advantage:

- Zero tolerance for prohibited goods, services, exploitation, illegal trade,
  and unsafe content.
- Terms acceptance before publishing or messaging.
- Profile review and reporting tools.
- Country-specific compliance readiness.
- Secure domain, HTTPS, audit logs, and moderation queue.

Avoid promising that all users are verified until verification workflows are
actually complete.

## Measurement Plan

Track:

- Organic search impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and average position.
- Indexed pages by country, industry, category, and profile type.
- Landing page conversion to signup.
- Signup conversion to profile draft.
- Profile draft conversion to published profile.
- Published profile conversion to paid subscription after free month.
- Source Finder searches by industry and country.
- Inquiries, chats, shares, downloads, and saved searches.
- Customer acquisition cost by channel.
- Trial-to-paid conversion by country and industry.
- Churn by country, industry, and acquisition source.
